# Jaggaer Software Quiz

Test your knowledge of the JAGGAER procurement platform with these 5 questions.

---

### Question 1

What is the name of JAGGAER's unified platform that covers the full source-to-pay spectrum?

- A) JAGGAER Pro
- B) JAGGAER One
- C) JAGGAER Suite
- D) JAGGAER Cloud

**Answer: B) JAGGAER One** — JAGGAER One is the company's fully integrated suite covering the entire source-to-pay lifecycle for managing both direct and indirect spend categories.

---

### Question 2

Which four software modules make up JAGGAER's Procure-to-Pay (P2P) workflow?

- A) Sourcing, Contracts, Analytics, Payments
- B) eProcurement, Supply Chain Collaboration, Invoicing, Payments
- C) Budgeting, Ordering, Receiving, Reporting
- D) Catalog Management, Approvals, Receipts, Auditing

**Answer: B) eProcurement, Supply Chain Collaboration, Invoicing, Payments** — These four modules are orchestrated together with guided buying, dashboards, and other facilities to streamline the procure-to-pay process.

---

### Question 3

What type of procurement model does JAGGAER primarily operate under?

- A) On-premises perpetual license
- B) Open-source community edition
- C) Cloud-based SaaS (Software as a Service)
- D) Desktop application with manual updates

**Answer: C) Cloud-based SaaS (Software as a Service)** — JAGGAER is a cloud-based procurement platform delivered as a SaaS solution, enabling organizations to streamline and automate the entire source-to-pay cycle.

---

### Question 4

What was JAGGAER formerly known as before its rebranding?

- A) Ariba
- B) Coupa
- C) SciQuest
- D) Basware

**Answer: C) SciQuest** — JAGGAER was formerly known as SciQuest before rebranding. The company is headquartered in Durham, North Carolina, and was acquired by Vista Equity Partners in 2024.

---

### Question 5

Which of the following is a key AI-powered capability that JAGGAER uses to help procurement teams with contract reviews?

- A) A blockchain-based contract ledger
- B) A GenAI-powered chatbot for contract-related questions
- C) An automated contract printing service
- D) A manual clause comparison tool

**Answer: B) A GenAI-powered chatbot for contract-related questions** — JAGGAER's GenAI-powered chatbot accelerates contract reviews by delivering fast, accurate answers to contract-related questions, reducing review time for legal and procurement teams.
