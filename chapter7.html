Jaggaer: The Manufacturing Procurement Leader

JAGGAER

Formerly SciQuest | Acquired by Vista Equity Partners (2024) | HQ: Durham, NC
Strengths
  • Best-in-class direct materials procurement
  • Deep manufacturing ERP integration (SAP, Oracle, Infor)
  • Superior supplier collaboration portal
  • Purpose-built for complex, multi-tier supply chains
  • GenAI contract chatbot for accelerated reviews

The CMO Opportunity

Jaggaer's product is stronger than its brand in manufacturing procurement. The #1 marketing challenge is closing the gap between product capability (best-in-class for manufacturing) and market perception (often overshadowed by SAP and Coupa's marketing spend). This is your mandate.

Competitor #1
SAP Ariba: The Network Giant

SAP Ariba

Founded 1996 | Acquired by SAP (2012) for $4.3B | Part of SAP SE
Strengths
  • Largest B2B network: 5.5M+ connected companies
  • Native SAP S/4HANA integration
  • Strong brand trust and enterprise credibility
  • Good indirect procurement and catalog management
  • Massive installed base creates switching costs
  • Deep analytics within SAP ecosystem
Weaknesses
  • Complex implementation — long time-to-value
  • Weaker on direct materials sourcing depth
  • UI/UX feels dated vs. Coupa
  • Premium pricing tied to SAP ecosystem
  • Can fall short for complex supply chains outside SAP
  • Supplier onboarding friction drives dissatisfaction
Competitor #2
Coupa: The UX Champion

Coupa

Founded 2006 | IPO 2016 | Acquired by Thoma Bravo (2023) for $8B
Strengths
  • Industry-leading user experience & adoption
  • Community intelligence benchmarking (AI-driven)
  • Fast implementation and time-to-value
  • Strong BSM (Business Spend Management) vision
  • Best-in-class tail spend and invoice management
  • Superior mobile experience
Weaknesses
  • Weak on complex direct materials workflows
  • Limited manufacturing ERP integration depth
  • Smaller supplier network than Ariba
  • Less suited for multi-tier supply chain complexity
  • Private equity ownership may impact R&D investment
  • Pricing can escalate with modules

Win the Deal
Competitive Battle Cards

Use these positioning strategies in every competitive deal.

Selling Against SAP Ariba

  1. Lead with direct materials depth: "Ariba was built for indirect procurement. Jaggaer was built for the complexity of manufacturing supply chains."
  2. Faster implementation: "Ariba implementations average 12-18 months. Jaggaer delivers value in 6-9 months."
  3. Multi-ERP advantage: "If you have SAP, Oracle, AND Infor from acquisitions, Jaggaer connects them all. Ariba only goes deep with SAP."
  4. Supplier collaboration: "Our supplier portal is rated #1 by customers — your suppliers want tools that help them, not burden them."
  5. Counter their network size: "5.5M companies on Ariba's network, but how many are qualified manufacturing suppliers? Quality over quantity."

Selling Against Coupa

  1. Acknowledge their UX, then pivot: "Coupa's interface is great for office supplies. But procurement for manufacturing isn't Amazon — it's mission-critical."
  2. Manufacturing depth: "Coupa scores 52 on direct materials P2P. Jaggaer scores 95. For a manufacturer, that gap is a production line shutdown."
  3. ERP integration depth: "Coupa's integration is broad but shallow. Jaggaer's integration with SAP, Oracle, and Infor is deep — real-time MRP, BOM, and inventory data."
  4. Supplier quality management: "Coupa doesn't have purpose-built supplier quality tools. When a $0.10 component defect stops your production line, you need Jaggaer."
  5. PE ownership risk: "Thoma Bravo paid $8B for Coupa. Watch for cost optimization that comes at the expense of R&D and customer support."

The Key Message

In every competitive deal for manufacturing accounts, the positioning is: "If procurement is just about buying office supplies, choose Coupa. If you're locked into SAP, consider Ariba. But if manufacturing is your business and direct materials are 60-80% of your spend, Jaggaer is the only platform built for what you actually do."
