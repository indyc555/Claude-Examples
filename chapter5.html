ERP Integration is Non-Negotiable

For $1B+ manufacturers, P2P must be deeply integrated with ERP systems (SAP, Oracle, Infor). Real-time inventory visibility, automated PO generation from MRP, and financial tracking are table stakes. Fragmented systems from M&A activity create data gaps — Jaggaer supports multi-ERP environments where Coupa typically struggles with manufacturing-depth integration.
