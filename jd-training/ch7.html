Sourcing: RFQ & eAuctions

Learn how JD's Sourcing module digitizes competitive bidding — from RFQ creation to reverse auctions, BOM-based sourcing, and award analysis.

Overview

What is Sourcing in JD?

JD's Sourcing module handles competitive procurement events — situations where a buyer wants bids from multiple suppliers and selects the best offer. This is distinct from the supply chain modules (POM, DCO, VMI) which manage ongoing supply from existing vendors.

Common use cases: new supplier qualification, contract renewal, cost optimization via auction, BOM-based sourcing for new product launches, and emergency alternate sourcing.

RFQ Process

RFQ Lifecycle — 7 Steps

1

Create RFQ Event

Buyer defines items, quantities, required dates, evaluation criteria, and submission deadline.

2

Invite Suppliers

Select from Approved Supplier List or invite open market suppliers. JD sends notification emails.

3

Suppliers Submit Quotes

Suppliers access the event in JOSP, complete the bidding template, and submit prices and lead times.

4

Bid Analysis

Buyer compares all bids in the award analysis cockpit — total cost, weighted scoring, scenario modeling.

5

Clarification Rounds

Q&A forum and optional price negotiation rounds before final award decision.

6

Award Decision

Buyer awards to one or multiple suppliers, with optional lot splitting for BOM items.

7

PO / Contract Generation

JD generates purchase orders or supply contracts directly from the award — no manual re-entry.

eAuction Types

Three Ways to Run an eAuction

🔴 Reverse Auction

Suppliers bid their price down in real time to win the business. Buyer sets a hidden reserve price (maximum they'll pay).

Best for: commodities, standard components, well-specified goods where price is the primary differentiator.

Typical savings: 15–25% vs. initial quote

🟡 Soft Auction (Japanese)

Buyer sets price targets; suppliers indicate YES/NO to each level. Less aggressive than reverse auction.

Best for: strategic relationships where open competitive bidding would damage the partnership.

Typical savings: 8–15%

🔵 Sealed Bid

All suppliers submit a single best-and-final offer simultaneously. No visibility of competitor bids.

Best for: complex goods where quality and capability matter as much as price. Award based on weighted scoring.

Typical savings: 5–12%
Terminology

Key Sourcing Terms

RFQ
Request for Quotation — formal bid invitation. Suppliers submit structured price and commercial offers.
RFP / RFI
Request for Proposal (broader criteria) / Request for Information (market research, no binding response).
Reverse Auction
Real-time competitive event where suppliers bid prices down. Lowest (or best total score) wins.
Reserve Price
Buyer's hidden maximum price — will not award above this. Prevents suppliers anchoring to it.
Lot Splitting
Awarding different line items of a BOM to different suppliers — optimize at component level.
Award Scenario
Analysis comparing different award combinations — single source vs split, lowest price vs best weighted score.
BOM (Bill of Materials)
Structured list of all components needed for a product. JD supports BOM upload and multi-line sourcing events.
Approved Supplier List (ASL)
Pre-qualified suppliers eligible to participate in sourcing events — only ASL suppliers can be invited.
Sealed Bid
Single best-and-final offer submitted by all suppliers simultaneously — no competitor visibility.
Savings
Difference between starting/market price and winning bid. Core KPI for the sourcing team.
Business Value

Pain Points Sourcing Solves

⚠ "RFQs via email — bids arrive in different formats, at different times, impossible to compare"
JD provides a structured single-platform bidding environment. All suppliers use the same template — bids are timestamped, centrally stored, and auto-compared.
⚠ "No competitive pressure — suppliers know they'll get the business regardless"
Reverse auctions create real-time competition. Suppliers see they're losing and bid down — driving 15–25% average savings vs. initial quote.
⚠ "Comparing complex BOM bids takes days of spreadsheet work"
JD's award analysis cockpit lets buyers model award scenarios, compare total BOM cost across combinations, and generate the award decision in minutes.
