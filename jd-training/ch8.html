eProcurement & Catalog Management

Complete your JD training with catalog-driven procurement — CCMT uploads, OCI punchout, BMEcat standards, and approval-based ordering workflows.

Overview

What is eProcurement in JD?

eProcurement in JD refers to catalog-based buying — where end users (engineers, maintenance staff, production planners) browse an online catalog, add items to a shopping cart, and the order flows through an approval process before automatically generating a purchase order.

Primary use cases: MRO (Maintenance, Repair, Operations — spare parts, tools, consumables), indirect materials, and standard components from pre-negotiated supplier catalogs. JD's eProcurement module bridges direct and indirect procurement.

Catalog Types

Internal Catalog vs OCI Punchout

Internal Catalog (Hosted in JD)

Buyer uploads and manages catalog content in JD's CCMT. Suppliers provide data in BMEcat format. Items, prices, and images are stored in JD.

  • Fast search — all data is local
  • Buyer controls what's visible
  • Works offline / in low connectivity
  • Requires regular updates from suppliers

External Catalog / OCI Punchout

User clicks "Punchout" → redirected to supplier's webshop. Shops there, fills cart, cart data returns to JD automatically via OCI protocol.

  • Always current pricing and availability
  • Full supplier catalog range
  • No buyer content management effort
  • Requires live internet connection
Process

Shopping Cart & Approval Workflow

1

User Searches Catalog or Punches Out

Internal search: browse JD catalog. Punchout: redirect to supplier site, shop, return with cart data via OCI.

2

Add to Cart

Items land in JD shopping cart with pre-negotiated prices, catalog references, and supplier info.

3

Submit Cart

User submits — JD evaluates approval rules (by value, category, cost center) and routes to required approvers.

4

Approval Workflow

Approvers review in JD and approve/reject. Configurable: threshold-based, category-based, sequential or parallel.

5

PO Generated

Approved cart auto-generates a PO — sent to SAP via IDOC or managed within JD directly.

6

Supplier Fulfills

Supplier receives order, ships goods. GR posted → invoice matched → payment processed as normal.

CCMT

Catalog Content Management Tool

CCMT is JD's built-in catalog management system — the backbone of internal catalog procurement.

Catalog Upload & Import

Accepts BMEcat 1.2 and 2005 XML files from suppliers. Bulk import with field mapping and validation rules.

Content Enrichment

Buyer can add/edit item data, upload product images, standardize naming, and map to eCl@ss or UNSPSC classification trees.

Pricing & Contracts

Link catalog items to negotiated contract prices with validity dates. Price updates flow automatically when new BMEcat is uploaded.

Approval & Publishing

New catalog content can require buyer approval before going live. Multi-supplier catalogs are searchable from a single JD interface.

Terminology

Key eProcurement Terms

eProcurement
Catalog-driven, approval-based electronic purchasing for non-MRP items. Replaces phone/email/card ordering.
CCMT
Catalog Content Management Tool — JD's built-in system for managing internal catalog content from suppliers.
BMEcat
German XML standard for electronic product catalog data exchange. JD supports BMEcat 1.2 and 2005.
OCI
Open Catalog Interface — standard protocol for punchout connections between buying systems and supplier webshops.
Punchout
External catalog — user leaves JD to shop on supplier's site, cart data returns automatically via OCI.
Shopping Cart
User's basket of catalog items — triggers approval workflow when submitted.
Approval Workflow
Configurable rules determining who must approve a cart — by value threshold, category, cost center, or user role.
eCl@ss / UNSPSC
Product classification standards. eCl@ss = European. UNSPSC = global. Used to categorize catalog items for search and analytics.
MRO
Maintenance, Repair, Operations — goods needed to run production but not going into the product itself.
Maverick Spend
Off-process purchasing bypassing approved procurement — no PO, no catalog, no approval. eProcurement dramatically reduces it.
Business Value

Pain Points eProcurement Solves

⚠ "Engineers ordering parts via phone/email — no visibility, no audit trail, no compliance"
JD's catalog forces all indirect orders through a structured, auditable process with automatic PO generation — full spend visibility.
⚠ "Catalog data is outdated — wrong prices, discontinued items, supplier content is a mess"
CCMT provides structured content management with versioning, price validity dates, and OCI punchout for always-current supplier data.
⚠ "Approval for non-standard purchases is slow, inconsistent, done via email"
Configurable digital approval workflows route carts to the right approvers based on value, category, and cost center — with automatic escalation for delays.
