Overview

What is VMI?

Vendor-Managed Inventory (VMI) is a supply chain model where the supplier is responsible for maintaining the buyer's inventory between agreed MIN and MAX levels — not the buyer. Instead of issuing purchase orders, the buyer shares consumption data and current stock levels. The supplier then decides when and how much to replenish.

JD's VMI module enables this by receiving inventory snapshots from SAP, sharing real-time stock data with suppliers in the portal, and allowing suppliers to create replenishment orders — or automatically triggering orders when stock falls below MIN.

Data Flows

What JD Shares in VMI

📊 Inventory Snapshots

SAP sends current stock levels for VMI-managed materials to JD on a schedule (daily or more frequent). The supplier sees real-time stock in the portal.

📉 Consumption Data

How much of each material was consumed in production. Helps suppliers understand velocity and plan replenishment timing.

📈 Forecast

Planned future consumption from SAP planning. Helps suppliers anticipate demand increases and pre-position stock before stockout risk.

Terminology

Key VMI Terms

VMI
Vendor-Managed Inventory — supply model where the supplier manages buyer's inventory between agreed MIN and MAX levels.
MIN Level
Minimum stock target. When stock falls below this, replenishment must be triggered by the supplier.
MAX Level
Maximum stock target. Suppliers should not replenish beyond this level to prevent overstock.
Consignment Stock
Goods physically at the buyer's location but legally owned by the supplier — buyer only pays when consumed.
WMMBXY / WMMB
SAP IDOC types used to transmit inventory snapshots to JD for VMI processing.
Coverage Days
Days of inventory remaining at current consumption rate. Formula: Current Stock ÷ Daily Consumption. Key VMI KPI.
Safety Stock
Buffer inventory held to protect against demand spikes or supply delays. Factored into MIN level calculation.
AUTO_PO
JD configuration enabling automatic PO creation when VMI MIN is breached, without requiring manual supplier input.
Snapshot
A point-in-time inventory reading sent from SAP to JD — the mechanism for keeping supplier stock visibility current.
Replenishment Order
Order created (by supplier or automatically) when stock hits the MIN level, to restore inventory to target levels.
Business Value

Pain Points VMI Solves

⚠ "Buyer spends hours weekly calculating reorder quantities for hundreds of C-parts"
VMI eliminates this entirely — supplier monitors stock and replenishes automatically. Buyer only manages MIN/MAX targets.
⚠ "Production stops because nobody noticed a component was running low"
JD VMI sends automated alerts when stock approaches MIN — supplier AND buyer are notified before stockout occurs.
⚠ "Supplier overdelivers and we have no space, or underdelivers and production stops"
MIN/MAX levels with shared visibility give a clear contract for stock management — both sides see the same real-time numbers.
