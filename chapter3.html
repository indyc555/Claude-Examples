Manufacturing Example

Sourcing a new precision-machined component supplier for an automotive OEM: Start with an RFI to 20 potential suppliers to assess capabilities and certifications (IATF 16949). Narrow to 5 for an RFP with full engineering specs, quality plans, and PPAP requirements. Final 2-3 receive an RFQ for volume pricing. Total timeline: 4-6 months including qualification.

Key Manufacturing Certifications

ISO 9001 — General quality management. IATF 16949 — Automotive-specific. AS9100 — Aerospace. ISO 14001 — Environmental management. ISO 13485 — Medical devices. For direct materials suppliers, these certifications are non-negotiable gates, not nice-to-haves.
