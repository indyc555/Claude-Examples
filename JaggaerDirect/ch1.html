Overview

What is Jaggaer Direct?

Jaggaer Direct (formerly Pool4Tool / p4t) is a cloud-based supplier collaboration platform built specifically for direct procurement — the purchasing of physical goods that go into manufactured products. JD sits between a buyer's ERP system (typically SAP) and their entire supply base, acting as the digital hub for purchase orders, delivery schedules, VMI, shipping notices, invoices, sourcing events, and catalog management.

The key differentiator: JD is purpose-built for manufacturing and industrial supply chains — automotive, aerospace, electronics, machinery. It integrates deeply with SAP via IDOCs and supports complex manufacturing workflows that general procurement tools cannot handle.

Terminology

Key Terms Glossary

ERP
Enterprise Resource Planning system (e.g. SAP). Source of truth for orders, inventory, and materials master data.
IDOC
Intermediate Document — SAP's proprietary message format for exchanging structured data with external systems. The pipe between SAP and JD.
MRP
Material Requirements Planning — SAP's planning engine that calculates what to order and when, based on demand and current stock.
Supplier Portal / JOSP
The JD interface for suppliers. JOSP = JAGGAER One Supplier Portal — the modern React-based UI where suppliers view orders, confirm, invoice, and respond to RFQs.
Wengine
Workflow Engine — JD's event-driven automation layer. When business events occur (e.g. PO created), configured rules automatically trigger actions like emails or status updates.
Company Settings (CS)
Customer-specific configuration flags that enable or disable JD features per company. Controls everything from confirmation requirements to invoice workflows.
Pure
JD's modern cloud-native React frontend. Older functionality runs in the "Legacy" UI. Jaggaer progressively migrates features to Pure.
BPE
Business Process Engine — JD's internal ticket/messaging system for structured buyer-supplier communications about specific documents.
t-sync
Table synchronization — the real-time data sync mechanism keeping JD and the ERP aligned on master and transactional data.
POM / DCO / VMI / ASN
The four core supply chain collaboration modules in JD. Covered in depth in Chapters 2–5.

Business Value

Pain Points JD Solves

⚠ "Suppliers don't know what's expected of them"
JD's supplier portal gives suppliers a single place to see all POs, call-offs, and forecasts in real time — no phone calls needed.
📞 "We're drowning in emails and phone calls about order status"
Automated notifications and self-service visibility cut unplanned supplier contact by 60%+ — buyers focus on exceptions, not status checks.
🔄 "Our ERP and supplier data are always out of sync"
t-sync and IDOC integration keep JD and SAP aligned automatically — every confirmation, ASN, and invoice flows back in real time.
