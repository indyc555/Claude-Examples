Electronic Invoicing

Understand how JD digitizes the full invoice-to-pay process — self-billing (ERS), 3-way matching, credit notes, and payment confirmation.

Invoice Flows

Three Invoice Processing Paths

JD supports multiple invoice flows. In all cases the goal is identical: eliminate paper, reduce processing time, ensure accuracy, and comply with local regulations.

Flow A — Supplier-Submitted Invoice

  1. Supplier creates invoice in JD portal
  2. JD validates against PO and GR (3-way match)
  3. INVOIC IDOC sent to SAP FI
  4. SAP posts invoice and schedules payment
  5. REMADV sent back to supplier on payment

Flow B — Self-Billing (ERS)

  1. SAP posts goods receipt (GR)
  2. SAP auto-creates invoice based on GR data
  3. INVOIC IDOC sent to JD
  4. Supplier sees invoice created on their behalf
  5. No supplier action needed — verify only

Flow C — EDI Invoice

  1. Supplier's ERP sends EDIFACT INVOIC message
  2. JD receives and processes the EDI file
  3. Same 3-way match validation applies
  4. INVOIC IDOC forwarded to SAP FI
  5. Payment and REMADV as normal
Core Concept

3-Way Matching

3-way matching ensures every invoice is validated against what was ordered and what was received — catching discrepancies before they reach the payment run.

Terminology

Key Invoice Terms

INVOIC
SAP IDOC type and EDIFACT message for invoices — used in both directions (supplier to buyer and ERS/self-billing).
ERS
Evaluated Receipt Settlement — self-billing model. Buyer generates invoice based on GR; no supplier submission required.
Credit Note
A negative invoice — used to correct overpayments, process returns, or apply pricing corrections.
3-Way Match
Validation that Purchase Order, Goods Receipt, and Invoice agree on material, quantity, and price.
Tolerance
Acceptable deviation between PO and invoice (e.g. ±2% price, ±0% qty). Out-of-tolerance requires buyer review.
REMADV
Remittance Advice IDOC — payment notification sent to supplier after payment is processed, showing which invoices were paid.
Invoice Dispute
When supplier disagrees with a posted ERS invoice, or when an invoice fails matching. Managed via JD's dispute workflow.
Payment Terms
Agreed payment timeline (e.g. Net 30, 2/10 Net 30). Captured in SAP and displayed in JD for supplier visibility.
Straight-Through Rate
Percentage of invoices that pass 3-way match and are auto-approved without human intervention. Target: 90%+.
Archiving
Legal requirement in many countries to retain invoice data for 7–10 years. JD supports compliant digital archiving.
Business Value

Pain Points Electronic Invoicing Solves

⚠ "Processing paper/PDF invoices takes 15+ days and costs €15–25 each"
Electronic invoicing via JD reduces processing time to 1–3 days and cost to under €3 per invoice — with automated 3-way matching for straight-through processing.
⚠ "Suppliers invoice wrong quantities or prices — staff spend hours investigating"
JD validates every invoice against PO and GR before it reaches SAP. Mismatches are flagged immediately with clear error detail and routed for correction.
⚠ "With ERS, suppliers have no visibility into invoices created on their behalf"
JD shows all ERS invoices in the supplier portal with full detail — suppliers verify, flag disputes, and track resolution status in real time.
