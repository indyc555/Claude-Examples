Purchase Order Management (POM)

Master the complete PO lifecycle in JD — from ERP transmission to supplier confirmation, deviation handling, change orders, and automated goods receipt.

Overview

What is POM?

Purchase Order Management is the most fundamental module in JD. When SAP generates a PO (via MRP or manual creation), it transmits it to JD via IDOC. JD displays the PO in the supplier portal, where the supplier can view, confirm, or propose deviations.

Without JD, this means printing/emailing POs, receiving confirmations by email or fax, and manually re-entering data into SAP — error-prone and slow. JD eliminates all of that with a fully automated, auditable digital workflow.

Process

PO Lifecycle — 9 Steps

1

MRP Run

SAP's planning engine determines material requirements based on demand, stock, and lead times.

2

PO Creation

SAP creates the Purchase Order (transaction ME21N) with item, quantity, delivery date, and vendor.

3

ORDERS IDOC Transmission

SAP sends an ORDERS IDOC to JD — the PO is instantly visible in the platform.

4

Supplier Notification

JD triggers the Wengine — the supplier receives an email alert and portal notification.

5

Supplier Review

Supplier logs into JOSP and reviews full PO details: item, quantity, price, required delivery date.

6

Confirmation or Deviation

Supplier confirms the PO as-is, or proposes changes (different qty, date, or price = deviation).

7

Buyer Review (if deviation)

JD checks deviation against configured tolerances. In-tolerance = auto-accept. Out-of-tolerance = buyer review queue.

8

ORDRSP IDOC Back to SAP

JD sends an ORDRSP IDOC back to SAP, updating the PO confirmation dates/quantities.

9

Goods Receipt

Supplier ships; buyer posts GR in SAP (MIGO), or automated via ASN (see Chapter 5).

Terminology

Key POM Terms

ORDERS
SAP IDOC message type for outbound purchase orders — sent from SAP to JD when a PO is created.
ORDRSP
SAP IDOC for order response — the supplier's confirmation (or deviation) sent back from JD to SAP.
ORDCHG
SAP IDOC for purchase order changes — sent when an existing PO is amended in SAP (qty, date, price).
Deviation
When a supplier cannot confirm exact PO terms — they propose a different quantity, price, or delivery date.
Tolerance
Configured thresholds for acceptable deviations (e.g. ±5% qty auto-accepted). Out-of-tolerance requires buyer approval.
Confirmation Level
JD configuration specifying how many rounds of supplier confirmation are required per PO line.
GR (Goods Receipt)
SAP transaction (MIGO) recording that physical goods have arrived. Triggers invoice matching.
Acknowledgement Deadline
Configurable window after PO transmission within which the supplier must confirm. Overdue = automated escalation.
BPE Message
Internal JD communication thread between buyer and supplier about a specific document (PO, invoice, etc.).
Inbound Delivery
SAP document created when goods are expected — linked to ASN data from JD. Used to plan and execute GR.
Business Value

Pain Points POM Solves

⚠ "POs via email/fax — confirmations get lost"
JD creates a digital audit trail — every PO, confirmation, and change is logged with timestamps. Nothing gets lost.
⚠ "Supplier confirms wrong quantities without buyer noticing"
Deviation workflow + configurable tolerances alert buyers to out-of-tolerance deviations and require explicit approval before SAP is updated.
⚠ "Change orders are chaotic — suppliers act on outdated PO versions"
ORDCHG IDOCs push updates instantly. Suppliers always see the current version and must confirm the change — old versions are grayed out.

Advanced Topics

Going Deeper

Blanket POs & Call-Offs

Framework orders with delivery call-offs are managed via DCO (Chapter 3). POM handles individual, discrete purchase orders.

Price Lock Configuration

Some companies configure JD to lock prices (no supplier price deviation allowed). Others allow price negotiation via BPE messages. Controlled via Company Settings.

Automatic GR via ASN

When an ASN is received and validated, JD can trigger automatic goods receipt posting in SAP — eliminating manual warehouse entry. Covered in Chapter 5.

Multi-Level Approval

Some customers configure multi-step PO confirmation workflows in JD — e.g. operational confirmation + quality sign-off before ORDRSP is sent to SAP.

Key Company Settings

CS_PO_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED, CS_DEVIATION_TOLERANCE_QTY, CS_DEVIATION_TOLERANCE_DATE — control the core POM behaviour per customer.

Acknowledgement Escalation

JD monitors the configured acknowledgement deadline. If the supplier hasn't confirmed in time, the Wengine sends escalation emails to the buyer and/or supplier automatically.
