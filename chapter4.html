Supplier Scorecards
The QCDS Framework: Measuring What Matters

Quality, Cost, Delivery, and Service—the four pillars that separate world-class supplier programs from spreadsheet chaos. Every $1B+ manufacturer needs quantified, automated scorecards.

Quality Metrics

Target Defect Rate: <1% for critical components, <0.1% for safety-critical parts. Leading manufacturers like Toyota operate at <50 PPM (parts per million).

  • Inspection Pass Rate: Percentage of incoming lots passing first inspection without rework
  • Corrective Action Response Time: Average days from non-conformance report (NCR) to CAPA submission
  • First Article Inspection (FAI): Pass/fail rate on new part introductions
  • Warranty Claims: Field returns attributable to supplier quality

Jaggaer automates NCR workflows, tracks CAPA timelines, and aggregates quality data across plants for cross-facility supplier comparisons.

Cost Metrics

Price Competitiveness Index: Benchmarks supplier pricing against market indices and competing bids for the same commodity.

  • Total Cost of Ownership (TCO): Includes purchase price, freight, quality costs, inventory carrying, and disposal
  • Cost Reduction YoY: Annual negotiated savings vs. prior year baseline—target 3-5% for mature categories
  • Invoice Accuracy: Percentage of invoices matching PO pricing without discrepancies
  • Payment Term Compliance: Adherence to negotiated payment windows

Jaggaer’s analytics engine surfaces hidden TCO drivers like expedited freight from late deliveries, connecting cost to quality and delivery performance.

Delivery Metrics

On-Time Delivery (OTD) Target: 95%+ for standard materials, 98%+ for critical production line components. Every missed delivery can halt a production line costing $50K+/hour.

  • Lead Time Accuracy: Promised vs. actual lead time—variance should be <5%
  • Fill Rate: Percentage of ordered quantity delivered complete on first shipment
  • Expedite Frequency: How often emergency expedites are required—a proxy for planning reliability
  • ASN Compliance: Accuracy and timeliness of Advanced Shipping Notices for dock scheduling

Jaggaer tracks OTD against PO promise dates automatically, flagging chronic late deliverers and correlating delivery failures to production impact.

Service Metrics

Responsiveness: Average time to acknowledge and respond to RFQs, change orders, and issue escalations. Best-in-class: <4 hours for critical items.

  • Flexibility: Willingness to accommodate volume swings, engineering changes, and rush orders
  • Communication Quality: Proactive vs. reactive communication—do they flag problems before you discover them?
  • Innovation Contribution: Value engineering suggestions, new material proposals, process improvements
  • Collaboration Index: Participation in joint planning sessions, QBRs, and continuous improvement programs

Jaggaer’s supplier portal captures service metrics through structured surveys, communication logs, and collaboration activity tracking.

Risk Management
Five Dimensions of Supply Chain Risk

Risk is not a single variable. Manufacturers face an interconnected web of financial, geopolitical, cyber, operational, and ESG/compliance threats that demand continuous, automated monitoring.

Financial Risk

The Threat: Supplier bankruptcy, credit deterioration, or cash flow crises that interrupt your supply. The average manufacturer loses 3-5 critical suppliers per year to financial distress.

Key Stats: 1 in 4 suppliers show early warning signs of financial stress 6-12 months before failure. 67% of procurement teams discover supplier financial problems only after a disruption occurs.

How Jaggaer Helps: Continuous monitoring of supplier financial health through credit scoring APIs, Dun & Bradstreet integration, and automated alerts when a supplier’s risk score drops below threshold. Pre-built contingency workflows activate backup suppliers automatically.

Geopolitical Risk

The Threat: Tariffs, trade wars, sanctions, and regulatory shifts that reshape cost structures overnight. The US-China tariff regime added 7-25% cost to thousands of manufactured components.

Key Stats: 82% of organizations say tariffs affect their supply chains. 20-40% of a typical manufacturing supply chain is exposed to geopolitical risk factors.

How Jaggaer Helps: Geographic risk overlays on supplier maps, tariff tracking with automated duty calculation, and dual-sourcing recommendation engines that identify alternative suppliers in lower-risk regions.

Cyber Risk

The Threat: Ransomware, data breaches, and supply chain attacks that exploit supplier connections as entry points. The SolarWinds and Kaseya attacks demonstrated that attackers target the weakest link.

Key Stats: 431% surge in supply chain cyber attacks from 2021 to 2023. 62% of network intrusions originate from a supply chain partner. Average breach cost: $4.45M.

How Jaggaer Helps: Supplier cybersecurity assessments during onboarding, continuous dark-web monitoring for supplier data exposure, SOC2/ISO 27001 compliance verification, and automated risk scoring that factors in a supplier’s digital hygiene.

Operational Risk

The Threat: Capacity constraints, quality failures, natural disasters, and logistics disruptions that interrupt production. A single supplier’s factory fire can cascade across your entire BOM.

Key Stats: 45% of procurement leaders cite supplier disruptions as their #1 operational risk. Average disruption costs 6-10% of annual revenue. Mean time to recover: 22 weeks.

How Jaggaer Helps: Real-time disruption monitoring using news feeds and IoT signals, capacity tracking dashboards, automated notification chains, and scenario planning tools that model the impact of losing any single supplier.

ESG & Compliance Risk

The Threat: Regulatory non-compliance with UFLPA (forced labor), Modern Slavery Act, EU CSRD, GDPR, and sector-specific regulations (REACH, RoHS, conflict minerals) that carry criminal penalties and brand damage.

Key Stats: 73% of consumers say they would switch brands over ESG concerns. EU CSRD mandates supply chain sustainability reporting for 50,000+ companies starting 2025. UFLPA has blocked $2B+ in goods at US ports.

How Jaggaer Helps: Automated ESG questionnaires during qualification, continuous compliance monitoring against global regulatory databases, supplier self-assessment portals with evidence collection, and audit trail documentation that satisfies regulators.

Best Practices for Manufacturing Onboarding

  • EDI/API Setup: Configure electronic data interchange for POs, ASNs, invoices, and payment remittances—critical for high-volume direct material suppliers
  • Compliance Verification: Automated collection and validation of ISO certifications, insurance certificates, tax IDs, banking details, and regulatory compliance documents
  • Catalog Integration: Punchout catalog setup for indirect materials; price list validation against contracted rates for direct materials
  • Quality Agreement Execution: Define inspection protocols, defect thresholds, CAPA timelines, and escalation procedures before the first PO
  • Capacity & Lead Time Confirmation: Validate production capacity, standard lead times, and surge capacity commitments
  • Logistics Setup: Define shipping methods, incoterms, packaging specifications, and labeling requirements
Manufacturing Spotlight — Global Compliance Complexity: A $1B+ manufacturer onboarding a new fastener supplier in Vietnam must simultaneously verify: US CTPAT security compliance, EU REACH chemical restrictions, UFLPA forced labor attestation, local factory audit results, ISO 9001 certification, export license requirements, and customs tariff classifications.
