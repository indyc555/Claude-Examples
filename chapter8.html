Your Strategy
The CMO's Strategic Playbook

Four messaging pillars to build your marketing strategy around, plus target personas and content recommendations.

Pillar 1: Manufacturing-First

"Built for the complexity of direct materials procurement." Lead with BOM integration, supplier quality, and JIT workflows that Ariba and Coupa can't match.

Pillar 2: Integration Depth

"Connects seamlessly with your existing ERP landscape." Multi-ERP support (SAP + Oracle + Infor) is a unique advantage for M&A-active manufacturers.

Pillar 3: Supplier Collaboration

"Your suppliers want to work with you, not against your tools." Jaggaer's supplier portal is rated #1 — position this against Ariba's onboarding friction.

Pillar 4: Total S2P

"One platform from sourcing through payment." Jaggaer One covers both direct and indirect spend — no bolting together point solutions.
