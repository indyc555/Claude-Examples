Manufacturing Spotlight

In manufacturing, S2P is mission-critical. A single missing component can halt an entire production line. Unlike services companies, manufacturers must manage Bills of Materials (BOMs), quality inspections at receiving, MRP-driven demand signals, and JIT delivery windows — all tightly integrated with ERP systems like SAP, Oracle, or Infor. Over 450,000 electronic components reached end-of-life in 2023 alone, making BOM accuracy a constant battle.

Know the Difference
Direct vs. Indirect Spend in Manufacturing

Your marketing messages must speak differently to direct materials buyers and indirect procurement teams. They have fundamentally different workflows, pain points, and success metrics.

Direct Spend Characteristics

  • ▶ 60-80% of total manufacturing spend
  • ▶ High-value, low-transaction-count purchases tied to production
  • ▶ Requires BOM integration, engineering specs, quality inspections
  • ▶ Switching costs are very high — requalification, testing, production risk
  • ▶ Driven by MRP demand signals and JIT delivery schedules
  • ▶ Raw materials price volatility (steel, copper, plastics) is a constant concern

Indirect Spend Characteristics

  • ▶ 20-40% of total spend, but 80% of transactions
  • ▶ High-volume, low-value: office supplies, MRO, IT, travel, services
  • ▶ Decentralized — HR, IT, marketing, facilities all buy independently
  • ▶ 29% is off-contract (maverick buying)
  • ▶ MRO is critical: unavailable spare parts can halt production lines
  • ▶ Catalog management and guided buying are key to controlling spend

Why This Matters for Marketing

When selling to a VP of Direct Procurement at a $2B automotive manufacturer, talk about BOM accuracy, supplier quality scorecards, and commodity price hedging. When selling to a CPO managing indirect spend, talk about maverick spend reduction, guided buying adoption rates, and tail spend visibility. Same platform — completely different value propositions.

Know Your Battlefield
The Big Three: Jaggaer vs. Ariba vs. Coupa

A preview of the competitive landscape. Chapter 7 goes deep — here's the positioning at a glance.

JAGGAER
Best for: Complex Manufacturing & Direct Materials
  • Strongest direct materials procurement capabilities
  • Deep manufacturing ERP integrations (SAP, Oracle, Infor)
  • Purpose-built for complex supply chains
  • Superior supplier collaboration tools
  • AI-powered contract analytics (GenAI chatbot)
  • Formerly SciQuest; acquired by Vista Equity 2024
SAP Ariba
Best for: SAP-Centric Enterprises
  • Largest B2B network (5.5M+ connected companies)
  • Seamless SAP S/4HANA integration
  • Strong indirect procurement and catalog management
  • Weaker on direct materials sourcing depth
  • Complex implementation; long time-to-value
  • Premium pricing tied to SAP ecosystem
Coupa
Best for: Spend Management & User Experience
  • Industry-leading user experience and adoption rates
  • Community intelligence benchmarking (AI-driven)
  • Strong BSM (Business Spend Management) vision
  • Best-in-class tail spend and invoice management
  • Weaker on complex direct materials workflows
  • Acquired by Thoma Bravo 2023 ($8B)
